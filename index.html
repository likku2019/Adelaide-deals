        How to read sale signsEach offer below is labelled
        Extra off ticket
        "Extra", "further" or "additional" % off comes off the price already on the ticket, at the register. A $60 ticket with an extra 50% off costs $30.
        Up to · few items
        "Up to 50%" means only a few items get the full discount, often one rack in a corner. Most get less or nothing.
        Selected items
        Only part of the store is included. Ask which racks or styles before trying things on.
        Storewide
        Almost everything, but look for "not included in further sales" stickers and new-arrival exclusions.
        • Ask at the counter: "Is the discount taken off the ticket price?" It settles it in five seconds.
        • Check the style code on the tongue or box label (e.g. HB8150-802) on the brand's own website. Outlet "was" prices are often old full prices.
        • Outlet-only ranges are made for outlets. They're cheaper because they're simpler models, not because they're marked down.
        • Go on event weekends. The deepest outlet sales run on long weekends, school holidays and Black Friday; in between it's usually the normal outlet price.
        • Check your size. The biggest cuts are often on the very small and very large sizes.
